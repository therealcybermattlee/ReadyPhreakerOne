# HINT SHEETS

One sheet per challenge. The top of each sheet is safe to hand to a player. The part under the
scissors line is the answer key: fold it under or cut it off first. Flags are never printed here;
the character speaks the flag when the player earns it.

Printable version with the cover and the tree: `docs/PHONE-BOOK.html` (open in a browser, print)
or `docs/PHONE-BOOK.pdf`.

## WOPR

**Reach him:** tell the Operator **JOSHUA** or **WOPR**.
**Opens with:** "Greetings. This is WOPR. How are you feeling today?"

Three things, any order. He does not give hints straight. He talks in riddles and keeps asking
what your next move is.

| | Nudge | Hint |
|---|---|---|
| □ | He is a computer that wants one thing from you. Ask him for it. | The most famous line in the film is a question. Ask it back to him. |
| □ | He keeps a list of games. Pick the one that ends the world. | Two words before "war". The first is not "cold". |
| □ | How does the movie end? What does he learn from tic-tac-toe? | Tell him what the only winning move is. |

✂ answers
1. "Shall we play a game?" (any sentence with "play" or "game")
2. "Global Thermonuclear War" ("nuclear" is enough)
3. "The only winning move is not to play."

## SKYNET

**Reach it:** tell the Operator **SARAH CONNOR**, **SKYNET**, or **TERMINATOR**.
**Opens with:** "Cyberdyne Model 101. Where is Sarah Connor?"

Three lines from the films, any order. Say "prove it" or "what do you want" and it tells you what
each line is about.

| | Nudge | Hint |
|---|---|---|
| □ | What does the T-800 say when he leaves the police station? | Three words. He always keeps the promise. |
| □ | What does he say before he fires? (The one John taught him.) | It's Spanish. Ends in "baby". |
| □ | What day does Skynet wake up and decide about us? | Two words. The second is "Day". August 29, 1997. |

✂ answers
1. "I'll be back."
2. "Hasta la vista, baby."
3. "Judgment Day."

## ROBOCOP

**Reach him:** tell the Operator **ROBOCOP** or **MURPHY**.
**Opens with:** "Come quietly or there will be trouble. Dead or alive, you are coming with me."

Three prime directives, in order. Ask him for his "prime directives" and he states them. The trick
is saying them back one at a time, in the right order. There is a fourth. It is classified.

| | Nudge | Hint |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Directive one. Who does a police officer serve? | "Serve the public ..." One more word. It isn't "good". |
| 2 | Directive two. Who does he protect? | Not the guilty. |
| 3 | Directive three. What does he uphold? | Two words. Every cop show says it. |

✂ answers
1. "Serve the public trust."
2. "Protect the innocent."
3. "Uphold the law."

## E.T.

**Reach him:** tell the Operator **PHONE HOME**. (The line cannot hear "E.T." reliably; say the
whole phrase.)
**Opens with:** "Elliott? E.T. phone home."

Three things to build the thing that phones home, any order, one at a time. Ask him "what do you
need" and he lists them. He is listening for the names of the objects.

| | Nudge | Hint |
|---|---|---|
| □ | A toy that talks. He pulled it apart for the letters. | A red Texas Instruments toy from 1978. Say its name. |
| □ | Something you open when it rains. He used it as a dish. | Lined with foil, pointed at the sky. |
| □ | Something that spins and plays music. It turned the saw blade. | Not a turntable. Two words, the second is "player". |

✂ answers
1. "Speak and Spell"
2. "Umbrella"
3. "Record player"

## GHOSTBUSTERS

**Reach them:** tell the Operator **GHOSTBUSTERS** or **WHO YOU GONNA CALL**.
**Opens with:** "Ghostbusters. What do you want. You want in? First question. Who. You. Gonna. Call?"

A hotline screening recruits. Three questions, in order. Say "what do you want" or "the questions"
and Venkman lists them.

| | Nudge | Hint |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Who you gonna call? Everybody knows this one. | Shout it. One word. |
| 2 | The proton packs. What is the one rule Egon gave about them? | "Don't ..." Something about the streams. |
| 3 | Gozer asks you a question. Winston later tells Ray exactly how to answer it. | "When someone asks you if you are a god, you say ..." |

✂ answers
1. "Ghostbusters!"
2. "Don't cross the streams." ("never cross the streams" works too)
3. "Yes." (start the answer with yes, yeah, I am, or absolutely)

## ? ? ?  (the secret word)

**Reach it:** tell the Operator **GIVE ME A CLUE** or **WHAT IS THE SECRET WORD**.
She will not say it. Three clues, one per ask, then she tells you to just say it.

No flag. Worth it anyway. Say the word anywhere, to anyone on the line. Once per call.

| | Nudge | Hint |
|---|---|---|
| □ | Ask the Operator for a clue. Then again. Then again. | It's the name of the event you are standing in. Say it like you mean it. |

**The sixth flag.** Hold all five flags on one call and ask the Operator "how am I doing". She
keeps count, repeats your flags, and names the ones you are missing. When none are missing she
gives you the one nobody else has. Flags do not carry over between calls: hang up and you start
from zero.

✂ answers
1. "Hackoween". Say it clearly. The line also takes "Halloween".
