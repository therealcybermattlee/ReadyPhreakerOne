Ready Phreaker One · the payphone

That payphone works. It just doesn't go where you think.

Pick up the handset. You'll hear a dial tone. The dial tone goes nowhere until you whistle the phreaker's tone, 2600 Hz. Close counts. Wait long enough and the line will tell you what it is listening for.

Then the Operator answers. She connects calls. She knows who is on the line. Ask her. Say a name and you're through.

Five characters are waiting. Each one wants to hear certain things, and none of them will tell you what. If you know the movie, you know the lines. Get them right and the character speaks a flag. Write it down exactly.

Hold all five flags on one call and the Operator will give you a sixth that nobody else has.

Hints are free. Answers are in the back if you want to cheat :) lol

How it works

The dial tone goes nowhere until you whistle. 2600 Hz, the phreaker's tone. Close counts. Wait long enough and the line tells you what it is listening for.

The Operator is a person, sort of. Talk to her. She knows who is on the line and how to reach them. She will not tell you the secret word, but she gives clues if you ask.

Say a name to be connected. Say main menu from anywhere to get back to her. Say goodbye to hang up.

Each character wants to hear certain things. They will not tell you what. Ask them what they want and listen. If you know the movie, you know the lines.

Talk over anything and it stops. You do not have to wait.

Ask the Operator how you are doing. She keeps count and repeats your flags.

Once per call. A character you have already beaten will not take you back. The Operator will tell you who is still missing.

Stuck? Hints are free. Answers are in the back if you want to cheat :) lol

The sixth flag

Hold all five flags on one call and ask the Operator "how am I doing". She keeps count, repeats your flags, and names the ones you are missing. When none are missing, she gives you the one nobody else has.

Flags do not carry over between calls. If you hang up, you start from zero. A call can run a long time. Pace yourself.
